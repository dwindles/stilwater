# Stilwater roadmap

## Done

- **Online:** our own server (Stilwater Online), in-game sign-in (automatic with the saved login), profiles with pictures, and profile looks (crew tag, title, banner colour, name colour and effect).
- **Friends:** the Insert menu (friends, joining without invites, notifications), Join buttons in the launcher.
- **Launcher:** redesign, animated ambience, Mods tab with share codes, Upload and Update buttons, zero-setup installer, "Your game" (time played, folder shortcuts, recent crash reports to copy), Play starts another copy while the game runs.
- **More than 2 players (first version):** friends past the co-op partner are characters in everyone's world; name tags over everyone's head.
- **Up to 4 in the game's own co-op:** the player cap, co-op memory and the session's room raised, co-op's two-player settings kept for more, and what missions do to your partner done for everyone (teleports, weapons, "either player" triggers, seats, a step apart instead of on one spot). Every player on the minimap and pause map, styled name tags over every player, going down near anyone and nobody bleeding out while someone can revive, cutscenes and mission starts for everyone, a joining player's game following the host, the host locked as host, and the host can kick any player. Joined players who can't reach each other through their routers go through the host (CoopRelay), joins wait their turn, one player leaving only takes what was theirs, the next player becomes missions' second player, and whoever starts an activity is the one in it. Up to four copies on one PC to test it alone; 3 copies seen together in one world (2026-11-03).
- **Clothes:** Supreme tees, Undercover jeans, Jordans, Cartiers, GTA jeans, baseball tees, Nike Techs, GLOCKIO belts; every clothing store sells every item.
- **Gameplay:** 20k ammo, saves never flagged as cheated, no co-op cheat pop-up, trainer switches that stay on, map icons you place, GTA Online controls.
